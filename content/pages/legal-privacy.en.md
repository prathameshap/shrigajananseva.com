Shri Gajanan Seva ("we", "us") is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organisation in San Jose, California. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and our activities, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

We do not sell or share your personal information. We have never done so and we do not intend to start.

## What we collect

**When you browse this website.** Aggregate, cookieless analytics only — page views, approximate region, referring site. No cookies are set for analytics, no advertising identifiers are used, and no profile of you is built. This is why you are not seeing a cookie consent banner.

**When you subscribe to the newsletter.** Your email address, optionally your name, your language preference, and a record of your consent including its date and time.

**When you use the contact form.** Your name, email address, subject and message.

**When you create a devotee account.** Your email address, name, and whatever you choose to add to your household profile — members, relationships, age bands, dietary or accessibility notes, and communication preferences.

**When you register for an occasion.** Party size, the names of those attending, and any dietary or accessibility notes you provide.

**When you donate.** Amount, fund, date, and the details needed to issue a receipt. Card details are handled entirely by our payment processor and never reach our servers.

**When you volunteer.** Shifts, verified hours, and for students under 18, guardian consent records.

## Why we collect it

| Purpose | Information used |
|---|---|
| Running occasions — prasad counts, seating, safety | RSVP and household data |
| Issuing donation receipts and annual tax statements | Giving records |
| Sending the newsletter you asked for | Email address and consent record |
| Replying to your enquiry | Contact form submission |
| Issuing service-hours letters | Volunteer shift and hours records |
| Meeting our legal obligations as a non-profit | Financial records |

We do not use your information for any purpose other than the one you gave it for.

## How long we keep it

- **Donation and financial records** — seven years, as required for non-profit accounting and tax purposes.
- **Newsletter subscription** — until you unsubscribe, plus a record of the unsubscribe itself so we do not re-add you.
- **Contact form messages** — two years.
- **RSVP and attendance records** — three years.
- **Volunteer hours and service letters** — seven years, so a letter can be re-verified years later if a school or college asks.
- **Account data** — until you delete your account.

## Who else sees it

A small number of service providers process data on our behalf, under contract, and only for the purpose we engaged them for: our website host, our email delivery provider, and our payment processor.

We disclose personal information to no one else, except where we are legally compelled to.

## Your rights under California law

If you are a California resident, the CCPA as amended by the CPRA gives you the right to:

- **Know** what personal information we have collected about you, where it came from, why we collected it, and who we disclosed it to.
- **Access** a copy of that information in a portable format.
- **Correct** information that is inaccurate.
- **Delete** your personal information, subject to the retention obligations above — we cannot delete donation records we are legally required to keep.
- **Opt out of sale or sharing.** We do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of. The link is provided anyway.
- **Limit use of sensitive personal information.** We do not collect sensitive personal information as the statute defines it.
- **Non-discrimination.** Exercising any of these rights will never affect how you are treated at the mandir.

To exercise any of these rights, use the request form on the [Your Privacy Choices](/legal/privacy-choices) page, or email us. We will acknowledge within 10 business days and respond within 45 calendar days, extendable once by a further 45 days where a request is complex — we will tell you if that happens.

You may use an authorised agent. We will ask for proof that you authorised them.

## Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. For students under 18 participating in the volunteer programme, we require written guardian consent before the first shift, and that consent can be withdrawn at any time.

## Photography at public events

Photographs and video are taken at utsav and public occasions and may be used in the newsletter, on this website and on our social channels. If you would prefer not to appear, tell any volunteer at the registration desk or email us, and we will honour it — including removing images already published.

## Security

Access to devotee and donor records is restricted to the trustees and volunteers whose seva requires it, and every access is logged. We will notify affected individuals and the California Attorney General as required by law in the event of a breach.

## Changes

We will post any change here and update the date at the top. Material changes will also be announced in the newsletter.

## Contact

Questions about this policy, or about anything we hold on you, go to the address on our [contact page](/contact).
