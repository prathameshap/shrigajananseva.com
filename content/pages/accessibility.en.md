A significant number of the people who read this website are over seventy and read it at 150% or 200% zoom. Several use a screen reader. Building for them was not an afterthought here — it decided the type size, the colour contrast and the decision not to put anything important behind a hover.

## What we aim for

**WCAG 2.2 Level AA**, across the whole public website. We believe we meet it. We are not claiming certification, because nobody has audited this independently yet, and saying otherwise would be dishonest.

## What is built in

- **Body text starts at 17px**, above the 16px minimum, with generous line height. The page stays usable and nothing is cut off at 200% zoom.
- **Text can be resized** without breaking the layout. We have not set a maximum scale on the viewport, so pinch-zoom works everywhere.
- **Colour contrast** clears 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text and interface elements, in both the light and the dark theme. Colour is never the only way something is signalled — a "live now" state carries a label as well as a colour.
- **Every control is reachable by keyboard**, in a sensible order, with a visible three-pixel focus ring that is never removed. Menus open on click rather than hover, so they work by keyboard and on touch.
- **A skip link** to the main content is the first thing in the tab order.
- **Headings are properly nested** — one h1 per page, no levels skipped — so a screen reader can be used to navigate the page rather than read it end to end.
- **Images carry alt text**, and decorative artwork is hidden from assistive technology rather than described pointlessly.
- **Reduced motion is respected.** If your system asks for less animation, the pulsing "live" indicator and every transition stop.
- **Dark mode** follows your system setting, and there is an explicit toggle if you would rather choose.
- **Marathi and English** are both first-class. Devanagari is served in Noto Sans Devanagari at a larger line height, because the default leading crowds the matras.
- **No cookie banner**, no interstitial, no pop-up, no autoplaying audio, and nothing that moves on its own.

## What we have not done yet

Written down honestly so you know where you stand:

- **No independent audit.** We have tested with VoiceOver and TalkBack ourselves. That is not the same thing.
- **The embedded map** on the Visit page is Google's, and its accessibility is theirs, not ours. The full address, cross street and phone number are always given as text beside it so you never need the map.
- **Library PDFs are not yet tagged** for screen readers. Several are scans of printed devotional texts. Ask us and we will read one to you over the phone or send what we have in plain text.
- **Video captions are incomplete** on older YouTube uploads. New uploads are captioned in Marathi.
- **No hearing loop** in the hall at the centre, and no large-print upasana booklets on site yet. Both are on the list.

## The centre itself

Step-free from the accessible parking bays through to the hall, with no steps anywhere on that route. Chairs for anyone who cannot sit on the floor — ask any volunteer. Accessible toilet on the same floor. Wheelchair space kept clear at the front during aarti.

Every aarti and the daily 7pm upasana are on Zoom, so you can take part without travelling at all.

If you would like a chair kept, help from the car park, or the wheelchair space reserved for a particular aarti, call or email a day ahead and a volunteer will meet you at the door. You do not have to explain why you are asking.

## Tell us when it does not work

This is the part that matters most. If something here is hard to use, we would rather know than not, and we would rather fix it than explain it.

Write to **info@shrigajananseva.org** or call **(408) 785-1450**. Tell us the page and what happened — "the donate page buttons vanish when I zoom in" is a perfect report. We aim to reply within five working days and to fix anything that stops you doing something, rather than putting it on a roadmap.

If you need any information on this website in another format — read aloud over the phone, in large print, or posted to you — ask and we will arrange it. There is no charge and no form to fill in.
