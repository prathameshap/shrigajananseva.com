California law gives you rights over your personal information. This page is how you use them here, in plain terms.

## Do we sell or share your information?

**No.** We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising — the two things the CCPA and CPRA are mainly concerned with. There is no opt-out link on this page because there is nothing to opt out of.

We have never done either, and we are not building towards it.

## What you can ask for

**To know what we hold.** We will tell you what personal information we have about you, where it came from, why we have it and who has seen it.

**A copy of it.** Sent to you as a plain file you can actually open.

**A correction.** If your name is misspelled or we have an old email address, tell us and we will fix it.

**Deletion.** We will delete what you ask us to, with one exception we cannot get around: donation records must be kept for seven years to satisfy the IRS. If you ask us to delete a donation record we will explain exactly what we are obliged to keep and delete everything else.

**To limit what we use.** We do not use sensitive personal information for anything beyond the purpose you gave it for, so there is nothing here to limit. If that ever changes, this page changes with it.

You will not be treated differently for asking. You will still receive the newsletter if you want it, you will still be welcome at the centre, and nobody will mention it.

## How to make a request

Email **info@shrigajananseva.org** with the subject line **Privacy request**, or call **(408) 785-1450**, or write to Shri Gajanan Seva, 2350 Paragon Dr, San Jose, CA 95131.

Tell us what you want and which email address or phone number we would know you by. We may need to check you are who you say you are before handing over a copy of anything — usually by replying to the address we already hold, which is the least intrusive way we know of.

**Our timings.** We acknowledge every request within **ten business days** and complete it within **forty-five calendar days**. If it needs longer we will write and tell you why before that deadline, not after it.

An authorised agent may act for you. Send us written permission signed by you, and we will still verify your identity directly.

## Changing what we send you

You do not need a formal request for any of this.

- **Newsletter** — every issue has an unsubscribe link at the bottom and it works immediately. Or reply to any issue with the word "stop".
- **WhatsApp community or channel** — leave the group or unfollow the channel in WhatsApp. We do not add anyone back.
- **Nothing else.** We do not run any other mailing, so there is no list of preferences to wade through.

## Analytics and cookies

This website sets **no advertising or tracking cookies**, and no analytics cookie either. Page views are counted by Vercel Analytics without any identifier being written to your device, which is why you have never seen a consent banner here.

If you would still rather not be counted at all, a browser-level "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal is respected, and any content blocker will stop the request.

## Requests we received

We publish the annual count of privacy requests and how quickly we handled them in the [transparency page](/about/transparency). The volume so far has been small enough that publishing it is genuinely straightforward.
