Shri Gajanan Seva is committed to making this website usable by everyone in our community, including devotees who are blind or have low vision, who are deaf or hard of hearing, who have limited dexterity, or who find dense text difficult.

A large part of our sangha is elderly. Accessibility here is not a compliance exercise; it is the difference between a grandmother being able to check tomorrow's aarti time by herself or having to ask someone.

## Standard we are working to

**WCAG 2.2 Level AA.** This site was designed and built against that standard rather than retrofitted to it.

## What conforms today

- Text meets or exceeds a 4.5:1 contrast ratio against its background, in both light and dark themes.
- The page remains usable, with no loss of content or function, at 200% zoom and at 400% on a narrow viewport.
- Base text is 17px with 1.7 line height, and scales with your browser or operating system text size setting.
- Every function is reachable by keyboard alone, in a logical order, with a clearly visible focus indicator.
- A "skip to main content" link is the first thing a keyboard or screen reader user reaches.
- Headings are properly nested, landmarks are used correctly, and form fields have real associated labels.
- Images carry alternative text; purely decorative images are hidden from assistive technology.
- Colour is never the only means of conveying information.
- Animation is minimal and is disabled entirely when your system requests reduced motion.
- The site works fully without JavaScript for reading content.
- Both English and Marathi are correctly marked up with language attributes, so screen readers pronounce each properly.

## What does not conform yet

We would rather list these than claim a conformance we have not reached.

- **Video captions.** Recordings on our YouTube channel do not all have accurate captions. Auto-generated captions handle the English portions reasonably and the Marathi and Sanskrit portions poorly. Captioning the most-watched recordings is in progress.
- **Audio transcripts.** The nam jaap and aarti recordings have no text transcript.
- **PDF documents.** Some library PDFs are scans without a text layer, which a screen reader cannot read at all. We are replacing these with readable HTML versions of each text.
- **Large-print booklets.** Printed upasana booklets are not yet available in large print at the mandir.

## At the mandir

The building is on a single level with step-free access from the parking lot to the sanctum. Accessible parking bays are nearest the entrance, there is an accessible restroom, and chairs are available for anyone who cannot sit on the floor.

A hearing loop is not currently installed.

If you need something arranged in order to attend comfortably, tell us in advance and we will arrange it. This is never an imposition.

## Tell us when we get it wrong

If any part of this site or of the mandir stops you doing something, we want to know — and we will treat it as a defect, not a feature request.

Email us or call, and tell us what you were trying to do, what happened, and what you were using. We aim to acknowledge within 5 business days and to give you a fix or a timetable within 15.

If we have not resolved it to your satisfaction, you may raise the matter with the trustees directly.

## Assessment

This statement is based on a self-assessment against WCAG 2.2 Level AA conducted during the rebuild of this site. An independent audit has not yet been commissioned; the trustees intend to arrange one.
