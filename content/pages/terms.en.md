## What this site is for

It is here so you can find out when the centre is open, what happens when, how to take part, and how to give if you want to. It is not a shop and nothing on it is sold.

You may read, print and share anything here. You may forward a page to somebody, print the timetable and put it on a noticeboard, or quote from it.

## Texts, recordings and images

The devotional texts and recordings in the [library](/library) are published so that devotees can use them. Download them, print them, share them, play them in your home, and use them in your own practice or in a group. We would rather you did than asked first.

Please do not sell them, and please do not republish them behind a paywall or with advertising attached to them. They were given freely and they should stay that way.

Photographs of the centre and of the murti belong to Shri Gajanan Seva. Ask us before using them commercially. Photographs that include identifiable people are not to be reused without their permission as well as ours.

## Accuracy of times and dates

We try hard to keep this accurate, and there is one thing you should know about it.

Utsav dates follow the tithi, not the Gregorian calendar, so they move each year and are sometimes not fixed until relatively close to the day. Where a date has not been settled we mark it clearly as unconfirmed on the page and in the calendar feed. **If you are travelling any distance for a particular day, call the centre first.** The phone number is on every page.

Times shown to you are converted into your device's timezone from the mandir's own — America/Los_Angeles. If your device's clock or timezone is wrong, the times we show you will be wrong too.
