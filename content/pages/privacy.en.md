Shri Gajanan Seva is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organisation in San Jose, California. This policy covers this website and the forms on it.

The short version: we collect what you type into a form, and nothing else. We set no advertising cookies, we run no cross-site tracking, we do not sell or share personal information, and we never have.

## What we collect

**When you use the contact form** — your name, email address, an optional phone number, the subject you chose and your message. We need these to reply to you.

**When you subscribe to the newsletter** — your email address, and your name if you give it. We send a confirmation email first and only add you once you click the link in it, so a mistyped address never ends up on the list.

**When you simply read the site** — aggregate page-view counts through Vercel Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights. These are cookieless. They record that a page was viewed, roughly where in the world from, and how fast it loaded. They do not set an identifier on your device, do not follow you to other websites and cannot be used to work out who you are. This is the specific reason this website has no cookie consent banner: there is nothing to consent to.

**When you ask us for something at the centre** — a sankalp in your family's name, a donation receipt, a student service-hours letter — whatever you give us in order for us to do that.

We do not collect payment card details on this website. Zelle, Venmo and your bank handle those, under their own policies.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. Under California law these are defined terms; we do neither, under any definition.
- We do not run advertising on this site.
- We do not use the newsletter list to solicit donations from people who only asked for the newsletter.
- We do not add you to anything because you filled in a different form. A guardian consent form for a student volunteer is used for that student's volunteering and nothing else.
- We do not use automated decision-making or profiling.

## How long we keep things

| What | Kept for |
| --- | --- |
| Contact form messages | Two years, then deleted |
| Newsletter subscriptions | Until you unsubscribe, then the address is removed |
| Donation records | Seven years — the IRS requires this of us |
| Guardian consent forms | While the student volunteers with us, then destroyed |
| Analytics | Aggregate only, never tied to a person |

## Who else sees it

Only the volunteers who need to in order to do the thing you asked for. Beyond that:

- **Vercel** hosts the website and provides the cookieless analytics.
- **Resend** delivers form submissions to us by email, if configured.

That is the complete list. No data broker, no advertising network, no analytics vendor beyond the above.

## Children

This website is not directed at children under thirteen and we do not knowingly collect their personal information through it. Where a student under eighteen volunteers at the centre, we hold a consent form completed by a parent or guardian, and we use the contact details on it only to reach that parent or guardian about that student's volunteering.

## Your rights

If you are a California resident, the CCPA as amended by the CPRA gives you the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to get a copy of it, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, and to opt out of sale or sharing — which in our case is already the position, since we do neither.

We will not treat you any differently for exercising these rights. You will still get the newsletter if you want it, and you will still be welcome at the centre.

See [Your privacy choices](/legal/privacy-choices) for how to make a request. We acknowledge requests within ten business days and complete them within forty-five days, and we will tell you if we need longer.

## Security

Everything on this site is served over HTTPS. Form submissions are delivered to a volunteer mailbox protected by two-factor authentication. We keep as little as possible, for as short a time as possible, because the most reliable way to protect information is not to be holding it.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and say what changed in the newsletter. We will not quietly broaden what we collect.
