California law gives you specific rights over the personal information we hold about you. This page is how you use them.

## We do not sell or share your information

Let us be clear about this first, because it is the question the law is most concerned with.

Shri Gajanan Seva does not sell personal information, and does not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We never have. There is no opt-out for you to exercise, because there is nothing to opt out of.

This site sets no advertising cookies, uses no third-party trackers, and runs cookieless analytics — which is why you have not been shown a consent banner.

## What you can ask us to do

**Know and access.** Ask what personal information we hold about you, where we got it, why we collected it, and who we disclosed it to. We will send you a copy in a portable format.

**Correct.** Tell us something we hold is wrong, and we will fix it.

**Delete.** Ask us to erase your personal information. We will, except where the law requires us to keep it — donation and financial records must be retained for seven years, and volunteer hours records are kept so service letters can be re-verified. We will tell you exactly what we kept and why.

**Limit sensitive information.** We do not collect sensitive personal information as the statute defines it.

## How to make a request

If you have a devotee account, the fastest route is your account's privacy page, where you can export or request deletion of your data directly.

Otherwise, email us with:

- Your name and the email address we are likely to hold for you
- Which right you are exercising
- Enough detail for us to find your records

Use the subject line **"Privacy rights request"** so it reaches the right person quickly.

## What happens next

1. **Within 10 business days** we acknowledge your request.
2. **We verify your identity**, proportionate to the sensitivity of what you asked for. For a deletion request we will ask for more than for a simple access request. We will not ask for more information than we need.
3. **Within 45 calendar days** we respond substantively. If your request is complex we may extend once by a further 45 days, and we will tell you before we do.

There is no charge, unless a request is manifestly unfounded or repetitive — in which case we will say so rather than quietly ignoring it.

## Authorised agents

You may use an authorised agent. We will require written proof of the authorisation, and may still verify your identity with you directly.

## You will not be treated differently

Exercising any of these rights will never affect your standing in this community, your ability to attend, to volunteer, or to receive prasad. It is your information. Asking about it is not a complaint.

## If you are not satisfied

Raise it with the trustees. You may also complain to the California Privacy Protection Agency or the California Attorney General.
